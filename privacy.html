Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Big 2 Online ("we", "us", "the app") handles your personal data. Big 2 Online is operated by Henry Charlesworth, an individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: support@big2online.app.

Big 2 Online is only for people aged 18 and over. Please also read our Terms of Service.

Offline practice

Practice against the computer runs entirely on your device and works without an account. Your year of birth from the age check, and app settings such as your card skin, are stored only on your device. If the device is online, ads may still be shown (see "Advertising").

Information we collect

When you create an account or use the online features we collect:

  • Account information — your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, via Google Firebase Authentication; a Firebase user ID; and the display name and avatar you choose.
  • Game and account activity — games you play (seats, stakes, results), your credit balance and full credit history, items you own and equip, XP, level, achievements, friends and friend requests, preferences, notification settings, the version of the Terms you accepted and when, and when you were last online.
  • Purchase history — when you buy credits, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat validates it for us. We receive a record of the transaction (product, price, time and store transaction ID) linked to your user ID. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Push notification token — if you allow notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for your device, used to tell you about friend requests and game invites.
  • Discord account (optional) — if you choose to link Discord, your Discord user ID and username, and whether you are a member of our Discord server (so we can grant the linking reward and add you to the server at your request). You can unlink at any time.
  • Crash and error reports — when the app or our server hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry. It contains technical details (stack trace, app version, device model, operating system, the screens visited just before the error) and your Firebase user ID, so we can tell how many players are affected. It does not include your email address or game content.
  • Device and advertising identifiers — Google AdMob uses your device's advertising ID, and the consent choices you make in the app, to serve and measure ads.
  • Technical and security data — your IP address and basic request details, used for rate limiting, abuse prevention and debugging. When you join through a friend's invite, we also record the IP address of your sign-up to detect referral fraud.

Inviting friends

When you invite a friend, the app gives you a personal invite link that you share yourself, using whatever app you choose. We do not email or message people on your behalf, and we do not collect your friends' contact details. If your friend signs up through your link, we record that they were referred by you so both of you can receive the referral rewards.

Service providers we share data with

We use the following processors. Each only receives what it needs to provide its service to us:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in (Authentication) and push notifications (Cloud Messaging).
  • Google Play — app distribution and payments.
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation (receives your user ID and purchase records).
  • Google AdMob — advertising, including Google's consent tools.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports (stored in Sentry's EU region).
  • Resend — sending transactional emails to your address.
  • Discord — only if you choose to link your Discord account.
  • Hetzner — hosting of our servers and database (EU).
  • Cloudflare — storage of our encrypted database backups (R2) and forwarding of email sent to our support address.

Other players can see your public profile: display name, avatar, level, equipped title and showcase items, game statistics, achievements, and leaderboard positions. Only your friends can see whether you are online and when you were last online.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of players or the Service.

International transfers

Some providers (for example Google, RevenueCat and Resend) process data in the United States or elsewhere outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK's adequacy regulations (including the UK–US Data Bridge) or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Active accounts — for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts — deletion takes effect immediately: we delete or anonymise your profile, email address, items, friends, achievements, push tokens, Discord link and preferences, and delete your sign-in account at Firebase. We keep the anonymised credit ledger and game records (with no name or contact details attached) because the ledger must stay complete for financial record-keeping and anti-fraud purposes.
  • Repeat sign-ups and bans — when an account is deleted we keep a keyed hash of its email address (not the address itself) for 12 months, so that the same email cannot claim the sign-up bonus again and so that an active ban carries over to a new account. After 12 months it is deleted.
  • Purchase records — kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires (up to 6 years in the UK).
  • Server logs — rotated automatically and kept for a short period, normally a few days.
  • Crash reports — kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Backups — encrypted daily database backups are kept for 30 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within 30 days.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. If you are in the UK or EEA, the app asks for your consent to personalised ads before they are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Manage ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Age

Big 2 Online is for adults only. The app asks for your year of birth before you can create an account and blocks anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using the app, contact us and we will close the account and delete their data.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. In the app you can:

  • delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account);
  • change notification and ad-privacy choices in Settings.

To get a copy of your data (in a machine-readable format), or for anything else, email support@big2online.app. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Deleting your account

To delete your Big 2 Online account and its data:

  1. In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. You must not be in a game or lobby at the time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  2. If you no longer have the app, email support@big2online.app from the address you signed up with, or fill in the account deletion request form, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm once it is done (within one month).

What is deleted and what is kept afterwards is described under "How long we keep data" above.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and secure WebSockets) and our database backups are encrypted. Access to production systems is restricted to the operator.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "last updated" date above and tell you in the app about significant changes.

Contact

Questions or requests: email support@big2online.app.
