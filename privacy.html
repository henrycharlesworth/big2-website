Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Big 2 Online ("we", "the app") handles your information. Big 2 Online is operated by Henry Charlesworth (hkmastergaming@gmail.com).

Information we collect

When you create an account or play online, we collect:

  • Account information — email address and display name, managed via Google Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google profile (email, name).
  • Game data — your credit balance, in-game purchases (cosmetic items, titles), achievements, match history, and level/XP.
  • Purchase history — when you buy credit packs, purchase records are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
  • Device and advertising identifiers — when ads are shown, Google AdMob may access your device advertising ID in line with Google's own privacy policy.
  • Technical logs — IP address and basic request metadata for abuse prevention and debugging. These are retained short-term.

How we use your information

  • To provide the game: authenticate your account, sync progress across devices, match you with other players.
  • To process in-app purchases via Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • To show advertising via Google AdMob.
  • To detect abuse, cheating, or violations of our terms.

Third-party services

Big 2 Online uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.

Children

Big 2 Online is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting hkmastergaming@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your profile, game data, and credit balance; purchase records may be retained where required by law or platform policy.

Data retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. If you delete your account, personal data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app or on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions? Email hkmastergaming@gmail.com.
